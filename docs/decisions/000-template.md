# Decision Record Template

Copy this file to `NNN-short-title.md` (e.g. `001-service-a-product-choice.md`) for
each significant modeling/business decision. Keep it short — this is a log for your
future self and for interview prep, not a formal doc.

## Title
One line: what was decided.

## Date
YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
What question or problem forced this decision? What options were on the table?

## Decision
What was chosen.

## Why
The reasoning — data evidence, tradeoffs, constraints — that led here.

## Alternatives considered
Other options and why they were passed over.

## Consequences / what this affects downstream
What this decision constrains or unlocks for later phases.
